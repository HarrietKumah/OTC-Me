# OTC&Me

Personalized over-the-counter medication guidance for the people you care about most.

[Try the demo](https://otc-me-connect.lovable.app/onboarding)

## What it is

OTC&Me helps people choose safer over-the-counter medications. It uses a person's age, life stage, symptoms, health conditions, current medications, and pharmacy profile to give recommendations that fit them, and it explains the reasoning in plain language.

## Why it matters

Most OTC labels say little about drug interactions or existing health conditions, so people often end up guessing at the shelf. Many adults live with a chronic condition, and OTC misuse and adverse reactions send more than 100,000 people to the emergency room each year. Pharmacists answer these questions all day, but they can't be everywhere. OTC&Me aims to give people a safe, clear answer before they buy, and to route higher-risk cases to a pharmacist.

## How it works

1. **Start with the person.** Choose who you're shopping for, such as yourself, a child, a pregnant family member, or an older adult.
2. **Screen for safety.** The tool checks age, pregnancy status, allergies, conditions, prescriptions, and contraindications.
3. **Get a recommendation.** It suggests suitable options, flags products to avoid, and escalates to a pharmacist when needed.
